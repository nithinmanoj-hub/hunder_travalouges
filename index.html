About HUNDER TRAVELOUGES

Travel planning designed around the journey.

HUNDER TRAVELOUGES is a Kannur-based travel planning experience focused on carefully structured routes, beautiful destinations, practical journeys and customer-friendly group travel planning.

We organise the journey around predefined package combinations rather than throwing random destinations together. The planner starts from the trip duration, then guides travellers through destinations that belong to the available planned routes.

Our current planner covers short one-day outings, overnight journeys and longer multi-destination circuits, with journeys starting and returning to Kannur. HUNDY GUIDE can explain the published routes, compare official combinations, remember the current conversation and move the enquiry to WhatsApp.
